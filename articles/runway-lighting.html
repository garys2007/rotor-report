Runway lighting and weather realism are the biggest gains in the newest simulator update

Night operations are becoming noticeably clearer in the newest simulator build, with more credible runway lighting, better visual cueing and less abrupt weather transitions.

For pilots doing low-altitude or instrument-dependent flying, these changes are among the most meaningful updates in the current release.
